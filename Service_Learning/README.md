# SERVICE LEARNING

==================================================

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This folder contains documentation of service learning activities undertaken as part of the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) programme at Assam Don Bosco University.

The activities focused on digital awareness, cybersecurity, and digital literacy, with the objective of sharing essential technological knowledge and skills with the residents of Don Bosco Snehalaya, Paltan Bazar, Guwahati, Assam.

## INSTITUTION DETAILS

**University:** Assam Don Bosco University  
**Department:** Department of Computer Applications  
**School:** School of Technology  
**Location:** Azara, Guwahati, Assam, India

## ACTIVITIES

### 1. SERVICE LEARNING – 2023

**Project Title:** Digital Awareness and Cyber Security

**Location:** Don Bosco Snehalaya, Paltan Bazar, Guwahati

**Team Members:**
- Anshuman Sarma
- Kashmira Borgoyary
- Anish Basumatary

**Activity Overview:**

The activity focused on improving digital awareness and cybersecurity knowledge among the residents. It also covered responsible online behaviour and electronic waste management.

**Key Topics Covered:**
- Digital Awareness and Digital Literacy
- Cybersecurity and Online Safety
- Malware, Phishing and Identity Theft
- Responsible E-Waste Management
- Safe and Responsible Internet Usage

**Individual Contribution:**

Anshuman Sarma delivered a presentation on digital awareness, explaining the importance of digital literacy, responsible online behaviour, and the benefits and risks of technology.

### 2. SERVICE LEARNING – 2024

**Project Focus:** Digital Literacy

**Location:** Don Bosco Snehalaya, Paltan Bazar, Guwahati

**Team Members:**
- Anshuman Sarma
- Owen Warlarpih
- Pavan Soibam

**Activity Overview:**

The activity focused on providing practical digital literacy training to the residents, helping them become familiar with essential digital tools and online services.

**Key Topics Covered:**
- Basic Digital Literacy
- Internet Connectivity
- Creating and Managing Google Accounts
- Effective Online Searching
- Introduction to ChatGPT and AI-Powered Tools
- Safe and Responsible Digital Behaviour

**Individual Contribution:**

Anshuman Sarma conducted training sessions on internet connectivity and Google account creation, helping participants understand the fundamentals of accessing and managing online services.
